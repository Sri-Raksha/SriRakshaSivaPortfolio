✦Just me

Hi, I'm Raksha 🌻

Born in Bangalore and raised in Chennai, India, I've always been curious about the world beyond what I know. I love to explore, experience and learn, from trying new things and meeting people to discovering places, cultures and ideas.

I've tried everything from basketball, swimming and dance to theatre, painting, keyboard and even abacus. Some stuck, some didn't, but I've always loved trying. 😭

I'm a district-level basketball player, NCC Best Cadet, IMO silver medallist, and former Theatre Club Secretary. I love movies, books, music, cooking, and editing random little videos for fun and posting them on my Instagram. Basically, I'm always looking for something new to create, try or experience.

On the professional side, I'm an Applied AI Master's student at Hochschule Heilbronn, with experience as a Business Analyst and internships across AI, Data Science and Machine Learning. I've also worked on research, presented at conferences, won two Best Paper awards, and contributed to two AI patent applications.

I'd rather be cringe than curious. I believe in putting yourself out there, trying things, failing, learning, and trying again.

Failures feel like a trampoline to me, the more you fall, the higher you can be pushed.

Studying abroad was a dream I had for years. Now that I'm in Germany, I'm here to experience the bigger world I once imagined, and hopefully discover a few dreams I didn't know I had.

Sri. Raksha. Raksaur. Ms. Siva. Different names. Different chapters. Still me. 🌻
