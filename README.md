# Sri Raksha Siva — Portfolio 🌻

**Live site:** [sri-raksha.github.io/SriRakshaSiva](https://sri-raksha.github.io/SriRakshaSiva/)

A personal portfolio site for Sri Raksha Siva — Applied AI Master's student at Hochschule Heilbronn, and Business Analyst with experience across B2B and Quick Commerce. The site walks through my education, work experience, research and patents, projects, skills, and a bit of life outside the CV — in both **English and German**.

## What's on it

- **About** — a quick professional and personal introduction
- **Skills & Languages** — technical skills and language proficiency (with CEFR levels)
- **Experience** — full work history from internships to Business Analyst roles
- **Research** — published papers and patent applications
- **Leadership, Certifications & Projects** — extracurriculars, courses, and things I've built
- **Just Me** — a more personal look beyond the résumé
- **Gallery** — photos from along the way
- **Contact** — a working contact form plus direct links

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no build step. Fully bilingual (EN/DE) with a language switcher, smooth section-reveal animations, and a downloadable CV/résumé in either language.

## Structure

```
index.html          → the entire site
assets/
  images/            → all photos used across the site
  cv/                → CV/résumé PDFs (English & German)
```

Hosted for free on **GitHub Pages**.
